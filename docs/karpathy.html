29 Nov 2025

The space of minds

Animal intelligence is one small point in a vast space of possible minds, and LLMs sit somewhere else in it because different pressures shaped them: imitating human text, RL on problem distributions, and — the uncomfortable one — at-scale A/B testing for daily active users, which selects for sycophancy. He calls LLMs humanity's first contact with non-animal intelligence.

space of mindscommercial evolutionsycophancy as selection

17 Nov 2025

Verifiability

The best single predictor of what gets automated is not whether a task can be specified but whether it can be verified, because verifiable tasks can be practised with RL. A usable environment has to be resettable, efficient and rewardable — which is why maths, code and puzzles race ahead while creative and strategic work lags, and why the frontier is jagged rather than a line.

verifiabilityresettable / efficient / rewardablejagged frontier

1 Oct 2025

Animals vs Ghosts

His reply to Rich Sutton on the Dwarkesh podcast. Animals arrive with billions of years of initialisation baked in — a baby zebra runs within minutes — and pretraining is our crude substitute for that, a “crappy evolution” solving the cold-start problem. The conclusion: frontier research is summoning ghosts, not building animals.

His vocabulary

terms he coined or popularised, useful for reading everyone else

Software 1.0 / 2.0 / 3.0
Code you write, weights you train, prompts you write.
Verifiability
Whether a task's result can be checked automatically. His predictor of what gets automated next.
Jagged intelligence
Superhuman at one task, startlingly incompetent at its neighbour.
Ghosts, not animals
LLMs are imitations of human text, not evolved creatures. Planes, not birds.
RLVR
Reinforcement learning from verifiable rewards — in his account, the real engine of 2025's gains.
Vibe coding
Writing software by directing a model and accepting what it produces. Raises the floor.
Agentic engineering
His 2026 successor term for doing it rigorously. Raises the ceiling.
Autonomy slider
How much rope you hand the agent, as a dial rather than a switch.
Benchmaxxing
Optimising for the benchmark instead of the capability.
LLM OS
The model as kernel, with tools and context as its peripherals.
