What each index answers

the same questions every time

  • Where do they publish?

    Every channel found, each one marked live, slow, or archive — with the feed URL, or a note that there isn't one.

  • How often, really?

    Counted, not guessed. Posts per year, the current gap between posts, and how long since the last one.

  • What are they on about now?

    The recent run, summarised, so you can tell whether the subject matter is still yours.

  • What should you read first?

    The canonical pieces, separated from the output that was never meant for a reader.

  • What should you discount?

    Who pays them, what they are selling, and which categories are marketing.

  • Is it news or understanding?

    Almost nobody is both. The verdict says which one you are subscribing to.
